# COP21

## The story

France spent almost two years preparing COP21.

The French side sent delegates and ambassadors to different countries,
built relationships and convinced ministers to put certain points
on the agenda.

Then, during the negotiation, a mistake happened...

## What this taught me

Negotiation starts long before the negotiation.

**Relationship → Trust → Ability to solve problems**
