HARDBALL TACTICS

Don’t fight the tactic. Identify the tactic.

Sometimes the other side deliberately creates pressure.

The first defense is simply recognizing what is happening.

⸻

EXCESSIVE ANCHOR

They start with an extreme demand.

Response: ask for justification.

Don’t immediately negotiate against yourself.

⸻

NEGOTIATING AGAINST YOURSELF

They make a proposal.

I reject it.

They remain silent.

I improve my own offer.

I just negotiated against myself.

⸻

RED HERRING

They introduce an issue that appears important.

I spend time negotiating it.

They eventually “concede” it.

Maybe they never cared about it.

⸻

BIG CONCESSION / SMALL CONCESSION

They give me something that costs them very little.

In exchange, they ask for something valuable to me.

The question isn’t:

“What is this worth to me?”

It is:

“What is this worth to them?”

⸻

LINKAGE

“If you want X, you need to give us Y.”

Sometimes legitimate.

Sometimes a pressure mechanism.

⸻

GOOD COP / BAD COP

One person appears unreasonable.

The other appears reasonable.

The “reasonable” person becomes the person I want to deal with.

⸻

HANDS TIED

“I would accept, but my boss won’t allow it.”

Maybe true.

Maybe not.

⸻

ULTIMATUM

“Take it or leave it.”

The question:

Can they actually walk away?

⸻

CHERRY ON THE CAKE

The main agreement appears finished.

Then:

“One last little thing…”

The timing is the tactic.
