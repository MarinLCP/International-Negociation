WHO / WHAT / HOW

Every negotiation can be looked at through three dimensions.

⸻

WHO

Who is involved?

Who negotiates?

Who has the mandate?

Who actually makes the decision?

Who influences the decision-maker?

Who supports me?

Who could block the deal?

Who is the spoiler?

⸻

WHAT

What are we negotiating?

What do I want?

What do they want?

What are our interests?

What solutions are possible?

What are my arguments?

What is my BATNA?

⸻

HOW

How will the negotiation happen?

Where?

When?

Who sets the agenda?

What is the deadline?

What information will be exchanged?

How will offers and concessions be communicated?

⸻

Why this matters

I naturally want to jump straight to:

“What do I want?”

But sometimes the most important question is:

“Who am I actually negotiating with?”

And sometimes:

“How are we going to negotiate?”

can completely change the result.

⸻
