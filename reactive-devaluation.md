Reactive Devaluation

The same offer can feel different depending on who makes it

Imagine two people make exactly the same proposal.

Person A is someone I trust.

Person B is someone I dislike or distrust.

The proposal is identical.

My reaction probably won’t be.

I may immediately look for problems in Person B’s offer.

This is reactive devaluation.

⸻

The problem

We don’t evaluate proposals independently from the person making them.

Our perception of the proposal is affected by our perception of the negotiator.

This explains why relationships matter even when the actual terms seem objective.

⸻

Connection with COP21

This is one reason the preparation done before COP21 mattered.

When something goes wrong, a trusted person has more room to solve the problem.

The same mistake from someone distrusted could become a much bigger issue.

⸻

Memory hook

Same offer. Different person. Different reaction.

Don’t underestimate the value of being trusted.
