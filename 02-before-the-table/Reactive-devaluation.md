REACTIVE DEVALUATION

Same offer. Different person. Different reaction.

THE IDEA

Imagine two people make exactly the same proposal.

Person A is someone I trust.

Person B is someone I distrust.

The proposal is identical.

My reaction probably isn’t.

I may immediately search for problems in Person B’s offer.

⸻

WHY?

We don’t evaluate a proposal completely independently from the person making it.

Our perception of the offer is affected by our perception of the negotiator.

⸻

WHY RELATIONSHIPS MATTER

This connects directly to [[cop21|COP21]].

If something goes wrong, a trusted person has more room to solve the problem.

The same mistake from someone distrusted can become much bigger.

⸻

THE NEGOTIATION IMPLICATION

Before asking:

“Is this a good offer?”

I should sometimes ask:

“How do I feel about the person making it?”

And the other side is doing the same thing with me.
